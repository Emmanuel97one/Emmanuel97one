## 🏢 EMMAPRO PHARMA EDITIONS & LICENSING

EMMAPRO PHARMA is available in **four professional editions**.

Each edition is designed for a different level of pharmacy operational complexity, business growth and workstation requirements.

### Edition Architecture

The editions use a **cumulative capability model**.

This means that a higher edition includes the functionality of the preceding edition and adds its own advanced capabilities.

**Pro → Pro-Plus → Pro Max → Ultra**

### Edition Comparison

| Edition | Included capabilities | Maximum Client PCs |

**Important:** The Main Pharmacy Server PC is separate from the registered Client PC allowance and is **not counted** against these limits.

---

### 🏢 1. Master Reseller (MRR) Program

**For established software distributors, technology companies and larger commercial partners.**

The Master Reseller program is designed for partners seeking to distribute EMMAPRO software across a broader market or build an approved reseller network.

### Master Reseller Opportunities

- 🌍 Develop an approved regional or commercial distribution network
- 💊 Promote and distribute EMMAPRO PHARMA
- 🤝 Establish direct relationships with eligible pharmacy customers
- 👥 Manage approved Authorized Resellers
- 📦 Receive a controlled License Allocation
- 🔄 Allocate part of available license capacity to approved Authorized Resellers
- 💼 Benefit from negotiated commercial terms
- 📈 Grow software distribution activity in an agreed market
- 📚 Use approved product and sales information
- 🛡️ Operate under EMMAPRO Tech's license-governance framework

### How Master Reseller Distribution Works

1. A prospective partner applies to EMMAPRO Tech.
2. EMMAPRO Tech reviews the application and commercial eligibility.
3. If approved, a Master Reseller agreement establishes the applicable distribution terms.
4. The Master Reseller receives an agreed, controlled License Allocation.
5. The partner may use available allocation for eligible direct customer licensing.
6. The Master Reseller may allocate permitted capacity to approved Authorized Resellers under its management.
7. Customer licensing and allocation activity remain subject to central governance and traceability.

Master Reseller status does not grant ownership of the software, trademarks, licensing infrastructure or unrestricted authority to create licenses.

### Who Should Apply?

- Software distribution companies
- IT solution providers
- Established technology resellers
- Business software distributors
- Commercial organizations serving pharmacies
- Partners seeking to develop approved reseller networks

Commercial margins, territories, allocations, support obligations and pricing arrangements are established by the applicable agreement.

---
